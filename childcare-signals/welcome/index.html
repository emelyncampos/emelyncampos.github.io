What to expect in a signal

Signals may include project or construction activity, regulatory or licensing activity, near-opening evidence, expansion or new-location activity, timing, context, public business or project contact details when available, buyer relevance, and the underlying public source.

One important limitation

A signal is not a confirmed opening, purchase intent, vendor need, procurement decision, or guaranteed sales opportunity. Public-source status should be re-verified before outreach or material business decisions.
