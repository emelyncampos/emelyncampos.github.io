Customer support

How can we help?

For questions about billing, delivery, a signal in your brief, corrections, or a refund request, contact Childcare Signals by email.

Support for the Texas Childcare Signals founding pilot.

Contact

Email emelyn@emelyncampos.com.br. Please include the name or email used at checkout when your question relates to a purchase.

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Billing and payments

If you do not recognize a charge, believe you were charged twice, or have a billing question, contact us first so we can review the payment and help resolve it.

Delivery questions

Founding Access includes four Texas briefs delivered over approximately four weeks to the email associated with the purchase. If you believe a brief was missed, contact support and we will review the delivery record.

Data corrections

If you believe a signal is inaccurate or a public source has changed, send the signal name and source link. We will review the underlying public record and correct future materials when appropriate.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled under our Refund Policy.

Need help?

Send us a concise description of the issue and we will review it.
