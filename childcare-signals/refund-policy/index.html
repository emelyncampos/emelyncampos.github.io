Refund Policy

We want the founding pilot to be a low-risk way to evaluate whether Childcare Signals is useful to your sales team.

Effective August 29, 2026

7-day refund window

If you are not satisfied with your founding-pilot purchase, email emelyn@emelyncampos.com.br within 7 calendar days of the purchase date and request a refund.

Eligible requests made within that 7-day window will receive a full refund to the original payment method.

After 7 days

After the 7-day window, refunds are generally not offered for briefs that have already been delivered. We will still review and correct duplicate charges, obvious billing errors, or a failure by Childcare Signals to provide the purchased service.

What happens after a refund

If a full refund is issued, remaining pilot delivery may be discontinued. Refunds are submitted to the original payment method. The time for the credit to appear depends on the card issuer or financial institution.

No subscription cancellation required

Founding Access is a one-time purchase. It does not automatically renew, so there is no recurring subscription to cancel.

How to request a refund

Email emelyn@emelyncampos.com.br with the subject Refund request — Childcare Signals and include the name or email used at checkout so we can locate the payment.
