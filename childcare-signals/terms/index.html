Founding pilot

Pilot Terms

These terms describe the limited founding pilot for Texas Childcare Signals. They are written to make clear what the service includes — and what it does not.

Effective August 29, 2026

1. What you are purchasing

Texas Childcare Signals — Founding Access is a one-time, four-week B2B information service. It includes four Texas briefs containing selected childcare market-entry signals identified from public sources and manually reviewed before delivery.

Each brief may include construction or project activity, licensing or regulatory activity, near-opening evidence, expansion or new-location activity, project context, timing, public business or project contact details when available, buyer relevance, and links to the underlying public sources.

2. Delivery

Delivery begins after payment confirmation. Four briefs are delivered over approximately four weeks to the email address associated with the purchase.

Signal volume varies with public activity and verification. There is no guaranteed minimum number of signals in a brief.

3. What a signal means

A Childcare Signals record is an informational signal based on public-source evidence. It does not mean a confirmed opening, purchase intent, vendor need, sales opportunity, procurement decision, or willingness to be contacted.

A construction record is treated as construction evidence. A licensing event is treated as licensing evidence. We do not intentionally collapse one stage into another.

4. No sales or business outcome guarantee

Childcare Signals does not guarantee leads, responses, meetings, revenue, customer acquisition, sales, or any other commercial result. The purchaser is responsible for deciding whether and how to use a signal.

5. Public-source limitations

Public records can contain errors, delays, amendments, estimates, or information that later changes. We manually review the source available at the time of publication, but we cannot guarantee that every public record will remain current or complete.

Before making a material business decision or contacting a prospective customer, purchasers should independently verify the relevant current source and comply with applicable laws, platform rules, and their own outreach policies.

6. Permitted use

The briefs are provided for the internal business use of the purchasing company. You may share them with colleagues inside the same organization for sales, research, planning, or business-development purposes.

You may not resell, republish, publicly distribute, sublicense, or commercially repackage the briefs or a substantial portion of their compiled content as a competing data product without written permission.

7. Payment and renewal

Founding Access is priced at US$49 as a one-time purchase unless a different amount is explicitly shown at checkout. There is no automatic renewal or recurring subscription attached to the founding pilot.

8. Refunds

Refunds are governed by the Refund Policy presented on the Childcare Signals website.

9. Support

Questions about billing, delivery, corrections, or the service can be sent to emelyn@emelyncampos.com.br. See the Support page for more information.

10. Future versions

The founding pilot is a validation-stage service and may change in future versions. Any material change to pricing, renewal, scope, or delivery applies only to future purchases unless the purchaser expressly agrees otherwise.
