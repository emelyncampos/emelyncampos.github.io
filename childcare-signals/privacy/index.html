Privacy Notice

This notice explains the limited personal information Childcare Signals uses to operate the founding pilot.

Effective August 29, 2026

Information we collect

We may receive the name, email address, company name, billing-related metadata, purchase details, and communications you provide in connection with the founding pilot.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Childcare Signals does not receive or store your full payment-card number. Stripe handles payment information under its own privacy and security practices.

How we use information

  • To process and confirm purchases.
  • To deliver the four-week founding pilot and related service messages.
  • To provide customer support and process refund requests.
  • To maintain billing, fraud-prevention, compliance, and business records.
  • To improve the service using aggregated or non-sensitive operational feedback.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the service, such as payment processing, email delivery, website hosting, or legal and compliance obligations.

Public-source business information

Childcare Signals also researches public business and government records for the product itself. The service is designed around public-source commercial and regulatory information; it is not intended to collect or sell sensitive personal data.

Retention

Purchase, support, and business records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and compliance purposes.

Your requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your purchase or to request a correction or deletion where applicable and legally permitted.
