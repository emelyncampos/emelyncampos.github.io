A REAL TEXAS EXAMPLE

Public data becomes more useful when the commercial timing is obvious.

On July 28, 2026, Texas TDLR registered a project called Fate Daycare SH 66, with facility name TLE Daycare, as new construction of a daycare facility in Fate, Texas.

The public record lists a planned construction start of September 21, 2026, an estimated project cost of $2.75M, and 10,000 sq ft of space.

DATA INTEGRITY

We would rather make a narrower claim than a wrong one.

A Childcare Signals record does not mean a confirmed opening, purchase intent, or sales opportunity. A construction record is a construction record. A licensing event is a licensing event.

Each signal is labeled according to what the underlying public source actually establishes, with the source retained so the receiving team can verify it directly.
