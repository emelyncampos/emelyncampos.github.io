One real example

The value is not secret data. It is finding the right public signal at the right time.

Texas TDLR registered Fate Daycare SH 66 on July 28, 2026 as new construction of a daycare facility in Fate, Texas. The public record lists a planned construction start of September 21, 2026, an estimated project cost of $2.75M and 10,000 sq ft of space.

What this tells a vendor

A material childcare project exists and has a public construction timeline.

What it does not tell us

That the center has opened, selected vendors, or intends to buy from anyone.

Who it’s for

The same center can become relevant to different sellers at different times.

Childcare Signals is being tested with vendors whose sales process benefits from knowing about a center before it is simply another mature listing in a database.

Childcare Software

Data integrity

Useful because it stays honest.

A Childcare Signals record does not mean a confirmed opening, purchase intent, or sales opportunity. A construction record is a construction record. A licensing event is a licensing event.

Each signal is labeled according to what the underlying public source actually establishes, with the source retained so the receiving team can verify it directly.
