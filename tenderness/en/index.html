BEFORE YOU BEGIN

You might be
wondering…

Is this only a memory app?

Tenderness brings memories and family organization together: routines, calendar, health, appointments, and growth. You choose what makes sense to record at each stage.

Can I save reports and appointment notes?

Yes. You can attach documents and reports, prepare questions, and record advice received during appointments.

Does the app assess health or development?

No. Tenderness organizes information entered by families. Charts show recorded measurements and do not replace professional assessment.

Is the memory book printed?

The retrospective is a digital PDF file. You can save it and choose how you want to use it.

Where can I download it?

The app is in its final publishing stage. App Store and Google Play links will appear here as soon as they are available.
