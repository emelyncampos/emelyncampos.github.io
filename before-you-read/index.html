01

The moment before the reading

The hard part often arrives one sentence too late.

You open a passage you already know. Then a phrase lands differently because a four-year-old is beside you. A question is coming. A detail needs context. A theological tension is more complicated than the children’s Bible version made it seem.

The problem is not that Scripture needs to be softened. It is that the adult sometimes needs a few minutes of preparation before reading it aloud.

The principle

Prepare the parent.
Preserve the text.

Before You Read does not rewrite the Bible for the child. It prepares the adult to enter the actual passage with more context, more clarity, and more awareness of what may need careful explanation.

  • It does not replace Scripture.
  • It does not decide whether your family should read a passage.
  • It does not improvise theology in a live chat.
  • It does not flatten legitimate Christian disagreement.
  • It does not build a relationship with the child.

03

Depth without a paper

Short to read.
Not shallow underneath.

The default Parent Prep is meant to be scanned in roughly one to three minutes. That brevity sits on top of a deeper editorial process: text, context, interpretation, application, developmental guidance, sources, and uncertainty are treated as distinct layers.

When Christians interpret a difficult detail differently, the product should say so when that difference is materially relevant. When a point is uncertain, the product should not manufacture certainty for a cleaner answer.

04

How it is being built

Built with AI.
Not delegated to AI.

AI can assist research, retrieval, comparison, drafting, age adaptation, prototyping, interface work, and testing. But the product is designed around a different question: where should human judgment remain non-transferable?

No raw model output becomes canonical Parent Prep.

Editorial review, source checking, theological distinctions, uncertainty, developmental care, and the decision to publish remain human responsibilities.

05

Behind the project

A product about preparation.
And a case study in judgment.

Before You Read is being built by Emelyn Campos Bertolini | Lyn Campos as part of a broader exploration of what it means to build with the tools of the future without outsourcing the parts that still require human responsibility.

Here, that means using technology to make careful preparation more feasible — while keeping theological judgment, editorial accountability, and the family’s own responsibility firmly in human hands.

If the validation supports the job

Grow the library carefully.
Keep the method demanding.

The next step is not to generate every passage. It is to learn whether this specific kind of preparation deserves to exist — then expand only with the same passage-first, source-aware, parent-facing standard.
