How it plays

Three moves. One perfect fit.

Each room takes 30 seconds to 3 minutes. Build it, test it, see what failed, fix it, test again.

  1. 01

    Drag

    Pull furniture from the tray into the room. It snaps to a grid where every square is 60 cm.

  2. 02

    Rotate

    Tap again to turn it. Wardrobes need room to open, chairs need a table, the sink needs the plumbing wall.

  3. 03

    Test

    A little person walks in and uses everything. If it works, they walk out the door happy.

    TEST
The door hits the plant The chair can’t be pulled out Can’t reach the fridge PERFECT FIT ★★★

Failure is shown, never just told. Get it right on the first test for three stars.
